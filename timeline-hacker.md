# Annotated Hacker Timeline - 1986 to 2026

| Year | Event | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1986 | Hacker Manifesto (Phrack #7) | Defines hacker identity outside the criminal stereotype |
| 1988 | Morris Worm | 1st large-scale worm; modern incident response is born |
| 2010 | Stuxnet | 1st known cyberweapon against physical infrastructure |
| 2012 | Law 12.737 ("Carolina Dieckmann") | Criminalizes device intrusion (art. 154-A of the Penal Code) |
| 2014 | Marco Civil (Law 12.965) | 1st broad legal framework for the Brazilian internet |
| 2018 | LGPD (Law 13.709) | Regulates the processing of personal data in Brazil |
| 2021 | Log4Shell discovered | Systemic risk of vulnerabilities in open source dependencies |
| 2021 | Law 14.155 | Toughens penalties for intrusion and electronic fraud in Brazil |
| 2022+ | Rise of offensive/defensive AI | AI-generated phishing and AI-assisted defense in the same race |
